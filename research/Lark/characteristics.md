# Characteristics — Lark
Desaturated, slightly cool, brightened. Lifted exposure and shadows. Greens and blues slightly boosted but overall desaturated. Clean, modern, airy. Popular for nature/landscape posts. No grain.

## Validation Sweep 2026-10-21

- **Notes:** Exposure +0.40 with green -25 and lifted blacks is right, but blue +10 / aqua +10 saturation goes the opposite way from Lark washed-out blue sky; ideally those become luminance lifts, not saturation.
