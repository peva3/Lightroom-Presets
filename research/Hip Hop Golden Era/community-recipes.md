# Hip Hop Golden Era — Community Recipes

Starting points for the 1988-1996 East Coast rap look: warm flash negative, gold skin, brown shadows, visible grain. The shipped `Hip Hop Golden Era.xmp` sits mid-range on every panel; the ranges below show how far the look can be pushed before it stops being a photograph of a person.

## Reference Looks

- **Danny Clinch, *Illmatic* (1994)** — Queensbridge block, available light, a face that is confident rather than lit. Contrast comes from the scene, not the grade ([Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/photos/nas-photographer-danny-clinch-reflects-on-1994-illmatic-photo-session-photo-gallery/)).
- **Jamel Shabazz, *Back in the Days*** — full-length street portraits in flat daylight, gold jewellery reading as bright yellow, backgrounds kept ([Aperture](https://aperture.org/editorial/why-jamel-shabazz-is-new-yorks-most-vital-street-photographer/)).
- **Ricky Powell, party and backstage frames** — direct flash, hot falloff, imperfect framing ([powerHouse Books](https://powerhousebooks.com/books/public-access/)).
- **Kodak Ektacolor Pro Gold / Vericolor flash negatives** — the actual stock family; smooth highlight roll-off, forgiving skin, mild grain.

## Slider Starting Points

| Panel | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure | -0.20 to +0.15 | Flash frames are usually already hot |
| Contrast | +15 to +35 | Above +35 the gold turns orange-red |
| Highlights | -55 to -25 | Flash speculars need recovering |
| Shadows | +10 to +30 | Keep the background readable |
| Whites | 0 to +20 | Whites are for the flash, not the sky |
| Blacks | -25 to -5 | Stay above -30; this look is not crushed |
| Saturation / Vibrance | 0 to +8 / 0 to +8 | Keep the two within 5 points, or omit Vibrance |
| HSL Sat | Orange +8…+18, Yellow +5…+15, Green -30…-10, Blue -20…0 | Orange is the image; blue is the enemy |
| HSL Lum | Orange +8…+20 | Lifts skin without desaturating it |
| Color Grading | Shadow 25-40 / 5-15, Highlight 30-45 / 8-18, Blend 70-80 | Both ends warm; never cool the shadows |
| Grain | 15-30 / size 20-30 / rough 40-60 | 35mm, not pushed-3200 |
| Vignette | -25 to -8 | Cheap-lens falloff |

## Common Mistakes

- **Calling it Portra.** Portra launched in 1998, after this era. The correct ancestors are Ektacolor Pro Gold and Vericolor III, which is why the highlights here are warmer and less pink than a modern Portra grade ([Kodak E-190](https://125px.com/docs/unsorted/kodak/e190.pdf)).
- **Cooling the shadows for "cinematic" contrast.** A teal shadow kills the look instantly. Both wheels belong on the warm side of the circle (25-45°).
- **Pushing orange saturation past +20.** Skin goes red and waxy; use orange *luminance* to make faces glow instead.
- **Crushing blacks to fake age.** The reference prints have detail in the shadows; -15 is enough, and -30 or lower reads as a modern Instagram edit.
- **Grain plus Clarity.** Clarity and Texture sharpen grain edges into digital noise. Keep both at 0 and let Grain Roughness carry the texture.
- **Applying it to a wide landscape.** There is no skin to carry the grade, and the orange boost turns dry grass and brick into a single orange field.
