# Characteristics — Marrakech Souk
Vibrant market colors: rich reds, oranges, warm earthy tones. Deep shadows for covered market intimacy. Boosted red/orange/yellow saturation. Warm split tone. Slight clarity for texture detail. Inspired by Marrakech medina souks.
