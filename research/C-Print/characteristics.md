# C-Print — Reference Notes for a Lightroom Preset

A generic RA-4 colour print look: moderate contrast, gentle highlight roll-off, warm-neutral paper white and slightly softened detail. Not tied to a specific film stock; modelled on the print stage rather than the negative.

## External characteristics

- Print contrast is lower than slide film and higher than a flat scan.
- Paper white is warm-neutral (D-min tint), never pure display white.
- Colour is slightly desaturated relative to the negative, especially in shadows.
