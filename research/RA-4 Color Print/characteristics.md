# RA-4 Color Print — Reference Notes for a Lightroom Preset

RA-4 is the standard chromogenic colour print process (Kodak Ektacolor / Fuji CA papers). The look is defined by the paper's toe and shoulder rather than by any one film stock: deeper blacks than a screen scan, warm paper base, modest saturation, and a gentle highlight roll-off.

## External characteristics

- Contrast sits between a negative scan and a slide.
- Warm paper white with a slight cyan/neutral balance depending on the paper.
- Saturation reduced at both ends of the tonal range (subtractive behaviour).
