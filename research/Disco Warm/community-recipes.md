# Disco Warm — Community Lightroom Recipes

Community settings for a 1977–79 nightclub look, compiled from r/postprocessing and r/AnalogCommunity threads on "Studio 54 colour", flash-photography tutorials, and the settings posted with scans of 1970s club and party negatives. The common denominator is warm, hard and dark: amber subject, amber shadows, no cool colour anywhere.

## Reference Looks

- **Allan Tannenbaum, Studio 54 1977–79** — direct flash on skin, deep falloff, warm room glow behind.
- **Rose Hartman's party frames** — harder contrast, more blown speculars, saturated satin.
- ***Saturday Night Fever* (1977) dance sequences** — coloured wash and mirror-ball speckle; useful for the highlight behaviour, less so for the grade.
- **Pushed 400 colour negative party snapshots** — coarse grain, magenta-leaning shadows, clipped highlights.

## Slider Starting Points

| Panel | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure | 0 to +0.25 | Flash does the lifting; don't over-expose skin |
| Contrast | +15 to +35 | Flash hardness |
| Highlights | −55 to −30 | Protect sequins, mirror ball, champagne flutes |
| Shadows | +10 to +25 | Keep hair and suits from blocking up |
| Whites | +5 to +15 | Small speculars only |
| Blacks | −25 to −10 | Firm, not crushed |
| Temperature | 0 to +8 | Only if the scene is genuinely tungsten |
| HSL Orange | Sat +10 to +20, Lum +10 to +20 | Skin glow — luminance matters more than saturation |
| HSL Yellow / Red | Sat +5 to +15 | Warm accents |
| HSL Blue | Sat −15 to 0 | Push the room back |
| Color Grading | Highlight 30–40 / 12–22; Shadow 25–35 / 8–15 | Amber at both ends |
| Grain | 15–25 / 24–30 / 45–55 | Pushed 400 |
| Vignette | −15 to −8 | Flash falloff |

## Common Mistakes

- **Saturating orange without lifting its luminance.** The 1977 look glows; +25 saturation with no luminance change turns skin terracotta.
- **Adding teal shadows.** Modern nightlife grading expects teal; this era has none. Shadow hue below 20° or above 45° starts reading as a different decade.
- **Using Brightness/Exposure to "fix" a dark background.** The dark background is flash falloff. Raising Shadows past +30 flattens the structure that makes the look.
- **Stacking Clarity on grain.** Grain 20 plus Clarity +20 produces digital noise; the styleguide requires Clarity/Texture/Dehaze at zero with grain on.
- **Over-vignetting.** Beyond −15 the frame reads as a lens defect rather than a room falling away.
- **Pushing Temperature and the amber grade together.** Double-warming clips orange clothing and makes skin plastic.
