# Characteristics — Vietnam Rice Terraces
Layered green terraces in mist. Rich greens with high luminance. Reduced contrast for misty atmosphere. Negative dehaze to enhance mist. Cool shadow grading, warm highlights. Slightly lifted blacks for fog feel. Inspried by Sa Pa rice terraces.
