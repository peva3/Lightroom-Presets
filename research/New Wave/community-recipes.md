# New Wave — Community Lightroom Recipes

Community settings for an early-1980s style-press colour look, compiled from r/postprocessing threads on "80s magazine colour", flash-portrait tutorials, and recipes published with scans from *The Face* and *i-D*. The recurring formula is cold shadow, magenta highlight, hard flash and high exposure — pastel's opposite number.

## Reference Looks

- **Blitz Kids portraits, 1980** — direct flash, seamless or painted wall, make-up reading as flat colour blocks.
- ***The Face*, 1981–83 covers** — bright whites, cyan-leaning shadows, magenta in the type and the clothing.
- ***i-D* street portraits** — flash against whatever wall was available; the wall colour does half the work.
- **Early Duran Duran / Visage promo stills** — gel washes and hard rim light; useful for the highlight hue, not the saturation.
- **Newsprint reproductions** — misregistered cyan and magenta, filled-in blacks. The reference for the "printed" feel.

## Slider Starting Points

| Panel | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure | +0.05 to +0.30 | Flash frames are already bright |
| Contrast | +15 to +35 | Firm, not crushed |
| Highlights | −50 to −30 | Gel hotspots and chrome |
| Shadows | +15 to +35 | Black clothing detail |
| Whites | +10 to +20 | Crisp paper-white |
| Blacks | −15 to 0 | Keep some floor |
| Saturation | +8 to +18 | Punchy, but paired with Vibrance |
| Vibrance | within 5 of Saturation | Prevents selective colour |
| HSL Blue | Sat +10 to +20 | Cold shadow support |
| HSL Magenta | Sat +10 to +20 | The signature highlight hue |
| HSL Green | Sat −35 to −15 | Foliage and fluorescent spill out |
| HSL Yellow | Sat +5 to +15 | Warm accent, small doses |
| Color Grading | Highlight 300–320 / 8–15; Shadow 195–215 / 10–18 | Magenta above, cyan below |
| Dehaze | 0 to +10 | Print clarity; never with grain |
| Grain | 0 to 15 | Page reproduction is clean |

## Common Mistakes

- **Adding teal shadows instead of cyan.** The era's shadow hue sits around 205°, cooler and bluer than the 180° teal of 2010s cinema grading.
- **Letting the shadow grade carry the exposure.** This is a bright look; if you darken it to make the cyan visible, it becomes moody rather than pop.
- **Symmetric colour grading.** Highlight magenta at 12 and shadow cyan at 15 must not be equalised — the imbalance is what makes the magenta read as an accent.
- **Saturating magenta without watching clothing.** Pink and magenta garments clip first; pull HSL Magenta back before global saturation.
- **Dehaze plus grain.** Dehaze is a multi-scale contrast operator; combined with grain it produces crunchy noise. Pick one.
- **Warm white balance.** A Temperature push neutralises the cold shadows and the look collapses into generic "vintage".
