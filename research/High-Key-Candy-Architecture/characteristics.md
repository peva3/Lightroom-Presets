
# High-Key Candy Architecture — Characteristics

Creator-tuned preset (first 26 of `Presets/Creative/`). No prior research folder existed; this file was created during the 2026-10-21 validation sweep.

## Key Aesthetic
- Aggressive high-key exposure (+1.60 EV) with compressed contrast (-20) and lifted shadows (+75) so architecture reads as white-on-white.
- "Candy" palette: candy-red and orange saturation lifts, deep blue pushed brighter (+45 lum) and hue-shifted -35 toward clean sky blue, calibration blue primary -20.
- Cool/warm anchor grade: warm highlights H45/S10, cool shadow H210/S5.
- Negative clarity (-10) for a clean, poster-like surface; light grain (10) for tooth.

## Usage
Best for: white/minimal architecture, pools, tiled facades, product and interior work with strong colour blocking. Avoid on low-key or high-ISO night frames (exposure +1.6 will blow them).
