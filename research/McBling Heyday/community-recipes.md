# McBling Heyday — Community Recipes

Ranges for a 2003-2008 maximalist digital grade: bright, saturated, glossy, warm skin, clean of grain. The shipped `McBling Heyday.xmp` is loud but controlled; community versions of this look tend to be louder still and much less careful with whites.

## Reference Looks

- **Paparazzi red-carpet flash** — hot faces, black background, colour that survives JPEG ([CARI](https://www.cari.institute/aesthetics/mcbling)).
- **Myspace-era uploads** — over-sharpened, over-saturated, compressed.
- **Compact-camera JPEGs of the period** — measured around 102% saturation out of camera ([Reviewed](https://www.reviewed.com/cameras/content/fujifilm-finepix-f440-digital-camera-review)).
- **Early camera-phone frames (2002-2004)** — 0.3-1.3 MP, visible blockiness, wrong white balance ([Digital Trends](https://www.digitaltrends.com/phones/camera-phone-history/)).

## Slider Starting Points

| Panel | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure | +0.10 to +0.50 | Bright is correct; glossy is the point |
| Contrast | +15 to +35 | Above +35 the flash faces clip into flat white |
| Highlights | -25 to 0 | Keep some texture in white clothing |
| Shadows | +10 to +30 | Open shadows, CCD-style |
| Whites | +10 to +30 | |
| Blacks | -20 to 0 | 0 to -10 for the glossy look; -20 for the harder tabloid version |
| Saturation | +10 to +25 | |
| Vibrance | within 5 of Saturation | +15/+18 is the shipped pairing |
| HSL Sat | Orange +10…+25, Yellow +5…+20, Blue 0…+15, Green -25…0 | Orange does the skin, green is contamination |
| HSL Lum | Orange +5…+18 | Glow without bleaching |
| Color Grading | Highlight 30-45 / 5-15, Shadow 200-220 / 0-12, Blend 70-80 | Warm flash over a cool room; keep shadow saturation low |
| Grain | 0 | This decade is digital; grain misdates it |
| Clarity / Texture | 0 | Use Sharpness ~10, not micro-contrast |
| Vignette | -25 to -8 | Flash falloff |

## Common Mistakes

- **Adding grain "for character".** The reference cameras had none — they had compression and noise. Grain instantly reads as 1990s film.
- **Crushing blacks.** A -30 black point plus +18 saturation turns the image into a 1990s club flyer. The tabloid black in this era comes from flash falloff, not from the tone curve.
- **Letting Clarity carry the "gloss".** Micro-contrast adds grey halos around jewellery and text; the glossy look is saturation plus clean whites.
- **Saturating green.** Club fluorescents plus +green saturation produces a sickly cast that no amount of orange can rescue. Green is the one hue to pull down.
- **Using Temperature/Tint to warm it.** Warmth here is graded (highlight 38/10) so the preset survives any white balance; a WB shift instead tints the whites, which is the one thing a glossy image cannot afford.
- **Applying it to a low-contrast flat frame.** With no specular highlights to blow and no saturated objects to push, the grade just looks slightly orange and loud for no reason.
