# Earth Tones — Community Lightroom Recipes

Community settings for a 1970s earth-tone colour look, drawn from r/AnalogCommunity and r/postprocessing threads on "1970s colour", Kodacolor/C-41 film-simulation recipes, and the settings posted alongside scans of 1970s family prints. The agreement is that 1970s colour is *quiet*: warm, desaturated, and slightly under-exposed, with olive rather than emerald greens.

## Reference Looks

- **William Eggleston, *William Eggleston's Guide* (1976)** — brown rooms, parking lots, red ceilings. The benchmark for ordinary subjects in muted colour.
- **New Topographics (1975)** — Robert Adams and Lewis Baltz: flat light, weak skies, no romanticism.
- **Kodacolor II family prints** — warm, slightly magenta, low acutance, often faded toward brown after fifty years.
- **1970s appliance catalogues** — avocado, harvest gold, coppertone brown. Use them to calibrate the *hues*, not the saturation.
- **Wood-panelled interiors under tungsten** — the domestic version of the same palette.

## Slider Starting Points

| Panel | Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure | −0.20 to 0 | Consumer prints sit slightly low |
| Contrast | 0 to +15 | Flat; the palette does the work |
| Highlights | −45 to −25 | No sparkle |
| Shadows | +10 to +25 | Panelling and upholstery detail |
| Whites | −10 to 0 | Suppress speculars |
| Blacks | −15 to −5 | Firm, never crushed |
| Saturation | −10 to 0 | Global mute — not HSL-only |
| HSL Green | Hue −25 to −15, Sat −35 to −15 | Olive, dusty foliage |
| HSL Blue | Sat −25 to −15 | Weak skies and denim |
| HSL Yellow | Sat −15 to −5 | Muted ochre, not lemon |
| HSL Orange | Sat 0 to +10 | Keeps skin and wood alive |
| Color Grading | Highlight 40–50 / 5–10; Shadow 30–40 / 8–14 | Ochre above, brown below |
| Grain | 20–35 / 25–32 / 50–60 | Consumer negative |
| Vignette | −15 to −8 | Album page |

## Common Mistakes

- **Adding teal shadows.** That is a 2000s cinematic convention. 1970s shadows are brown or neutral, never cyan.
- **Over-desaturating greens until foliage goes grey.** The target is olive — a hue shift plus a moderate cut, not a kill.
- **Reaching for Clarity on top of grain.** Local contrast turns simulated grain into digital noise; the styleguide requires Clarity/Texture/Dehaze at zero whenever Grain is on.
- **Modern WB warming.** A Temperature push plus ochre grading double-warms skin. Let Color Grading carry the warmth.
- **Crushing blacks for "vintage".** Decade prints are soft, not contrasty; a black floor below −15 reads as a modern grade.
- **Saturating orange.** The palette's warmth is low-chroma; +20 orange immediately looks like a 2010s preset.
