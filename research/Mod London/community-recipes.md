# Community Lightroom Recipes — Mod London

Notes for adapting the **Mod London** preset. This is a *neutral, cool-shadowed* mid-1960s London editorial look: drab olive ground, graphic red and blue accents, pale skin and magazine-scale grain.

## Reference Looks

- **David Bailey, *Box of Pin-Ups* (1964)** — pale skin against white seamless, close crop, sharp light.
- **Terence Donovan, menswear on gasworks and power stations, 1960–1963** — industrial grey-olive backdrops, models in sharp suits.
- **Sunday Times Colour Section, from 4 February 1962** — Bailey's Shrimpton/Quant cover; the colour-supplement printing register.
- **Blow-Up (1966)** — the studio-and-street colour of the period, slightly cold, slightly grainy.

## Slider Starting Points

| Panel | Range | Contributes |
|---|---|---|
| Exposure | +0.10 to +0.30 | Open, reportorial exposure |
| Contrast | +10 to +20 | "Soot and whitewash" separation |
| Highlights | -30 to -45 | Pale skin and white seamless |
| Shadows | +15 to +30 | Shadow detail in coats and doorways |
| Whites | +5 to +15 | Clean press whites |
| Blacks | -10 to 0 | No ink-black anchor |
| HSL Green hue | -10 to -20 | Foliage toward yellow-olive (negative = toward yellow) |
| HSL Green sat | -15 to -30 | The drab London ground |
| HSL Blue sat | +5 to +15 | Graphic blue accent |
| HSL Red sat | +5 to +15 | Bus, postbox, lipstick |
| HSL Orange lum | +6 to +14 | Pale, clean, high-key skin |
| Color Grade highlight | 30–40 hue / 3–8 sat | Neutral-warm skin, not golden |
| Color Grade shadow | 200–220 hue / 8–14 sat | Blue-grey shadow |
| Grain | 10–20 amount / 20–28 size | 35mm enlarged to a spread (Sharpness 10 required) |
| Vignette | -5 to -10 | Tight-crop edge pressure |

## Common Mistakes

- **Flipping the Green hue positive.** Positive moves foliage toward cyan/aqua; the period look needs olive-yellow, so the value must be negative.
- **Pushing the shadow past 220°.** You leave "grey London day" and land in teal-and-orange cinema.
- **Warming the whole frame.** Sepia fog is a different era; mid-60s London is cold and neutral in the mids.
- **Adding global Saturation.** It revives the greens the preset is deliberately suppressing, and the drab ground is half the look.
- **Positive Clarity.** Press contrast came from printing, not from local-contrast micro-sharpening; clarity hardens skin.
- **Heavy grain on portraits.** 15 is already at the top of the usable range for faces; 30 reads as a pushed 1970s stock.
